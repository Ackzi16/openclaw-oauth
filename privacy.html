OpenClaw Personal

Policy

Privacy policy

This policy explains how OpenClaw Personal handles Google account data.

Scope

OpenClaw Personal is a private, single-user integration operated by the Google account owner. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

The integration may access Gmail messages and settings, Google Calendar events and calendars, and Google Drive files and metadata. The exact access is limited to the permissions shown on Google's OAuth consent screen.

How data is used

Google account data is used only to carry out requests made by the account owner, maintain the requested automation, and diagnose failures. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training public machine-learning models.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and operational data are stored on the owner's private OpenClaw server. Data is not sold. It is not shared with third parties except when needed to provide the Google services requested by the owner or when required by law.

Retention and security

Operational data is retained only for as long as the owner needs the automation or its logs. Access is restricted to the owner and the owner's server processes. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the integration uses access controls and encrypted connections.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop the integration at any time by revoking OpenClaw Personal in the Google Account security settings and deleting its credentials or stored data from the OpenClaw server.

Policy changes and support

Material changes will be posted on this page. Support is available through the verified support contact shown on Google's OAuth consent screen.

Effective: 29 October 2026
